## Risk Review Cadence

- Review at each phase start and exit.
- S03F-01 must explicitly review R-19, R-20, R-21 and R-24 before S03F-02 begins.
- Any Critical-impact risk becoming likely blocks downstream performance claims and may block the dependent correctness plan.
- Add evidence links and status in phase summaries; do not silently remove closed risks.
- New public benchmark claims require explicit review of R-07, R-08, R-09, R-15, R-17 and relevant multi-device/residency risks.
